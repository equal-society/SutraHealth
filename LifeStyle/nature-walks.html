Indoor Plants for a Pleasant Home Environment

Here are five indoor plants that can add greenery and contribute to a pleasant home environment.

Peace Lily

Spathiphyllum

A humidity-loving flowering plant that thrives in indirect sunlight. It has been studied for interaction with certain air pollutants under controlled conditions.

Spider Plant

Chlorophytum comosum

A beginner-friendly indoor plant that survives easily in indirect sunlight and adapts well to different conditions.

Money Plant

Golden Pothos

A hardy indoor plant that grows well in water or pots. It has been studied for interaction with certain air pollutants under controlled conditions.

Snake Plant

Sansevieria

One of the easier indoor plants to maintain, with studies examining its interaction with certain indoor air pollutants under controlled conditions.

Boston Fern

Nephrolepis exaltata

A lush indoor fern that adds greenery and beauty to your living space. It has also been studied for interaction with certain air pollutants under controlled conditions.
