Why Integrative Medicine Takes a Whole-Person Approach

Integrative medicine considers the person as a whole rather than looking at one health concern in isolation. It can bring together lifestyle, nutrition, movement, stress, sleep, behaviour and health education as appropriate to the individual.

Patient At The Centre

Integrative healthcare is built around partnership and individual context. A person's goals, daily routine, preferences and health needs can inform the conversation between the person and qualified healthcare professionals.

Lifestyle Influences Health

Daily habits such as food choices, physical activity, sleep, stress management and social environment can influence overall wellbeing. Lifestyle-focused guidance helps people understand which habits may be relevant to their health goals.

Whole-Person Approach

A whole-person approach looks at how different aspects of health and daily life interact. Integrative care can bring relevant lifestyle and wellbeing considerations into a broader healthcare conversation.

Supporting Healthy Change

The aim is to support healthy behaviours, informed decision-making and sustainable lifestyle change. Any healthcare plan should be appropriate to the person's circumstances and guided by qualified professionals when clinical care is required.

Examples of Integrative Healthcare

An integrative approach can bring together appropriate health education, lifestyle support and complementary approaches alongside suitable healthcare, depending on individual needs and professional guidance.

Lifestyle Medicine

Lifestyle-focused guidance can include nutrition, physical activity, sleep, stress management and behaviour change as part of a broader health plan.

Yoga Therapy

Yoga-based therapeutic practices may be considered when appropriate, with the practice adapted to the person and guided safely.

Nutrition & Health Education

Nutrition education and clear health information can help people understand lifestyle factors and make more informed day-to-day choices.

Integrative Medicine

Questions about integrative medicine, whole-person care and online consultation.

What is integrative medicine?

Integrative medicine is a whole-person approach that considers appropriate conventional and complementary approaches in a coordinated way.

What are examples of integrative medicine?

Examples may include lifestyle-focused care, nutrition education, yoga therapy or other mind-body approaches, health education and appropriate conventional healthcare.

Does integrative medicine replace conventional treatment?

No. Complementary approaches should not be used to delay appropriate medical evaluation or replace prescribed treatment without qualified guidance.

Is online integrative medicine consultation available?

Sutra Health has an online consultation pathway. The appropriate consultation and next steps depend on the person’s health needs and professional assessment.

How does lifestyle medicine relate to integrative medicine?

Lifestyle medicine can be one component of a broader integrative approach, including nutrition, movement, sleep, stress and behaviour change.

Can yoga therapy be part of integrative healthcare?

Yoga therapy may be considered as part of integrative healthcare when appropriate and professionally guided.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Integrative Medicine FAQs

Answers to common questions about integrative medicine and online consultation.

What is integrative medicine?

Integrative medicine is a whole-person approach that considers relevant lifestyle, health and wellbeing factors alongside appropriate healthcare.

How does integrative medicine work?

The approach starts by understanding the person's health context, goals and needs, then considers appropriate lifestyle and healthcare strategies with professional guidance where required.

What are examples of integrative healthcare?

Examples can include lifestyle education, nutrition guidance, yoga therapy, behaviour-change support, sleep and stress education, and appropriate health consultation.

What is an integrative medicine consultation?

An integrative medicine consultation is an opportunity to discuss health concerns, lifestyle factors and goals and understand an appropriate next step based on individual circumstances.

Is online integrative medicine consultation available?

Sutra Health provides an online consultation pathway. The appropriate consultation and support depend on individual needs and professional assessment.

Does integrative medicine replace conventional medicine?

Integrative healthcare should not be presented as a blanket replacement for necessary conventional diagnosis or treatment. Appropriate care depends on the person's circumstances and professional assessment.

What is the difference between integrative and alternative medicine?

Integrative medicine generally considers appropriate complementary approaches alongside conventional healthcare, while alternative medicine is commonly used to describe approaches used instead of conventional care.

How does lifestyle medicine relate to integrative medicine?

Lifestyle medicine focuses on health-related behaviours and habits, and lifestyle-focused care can form one component of a broader integrative healthcare approach.
