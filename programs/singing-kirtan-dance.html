Healing Through Music & Movement

Singing has many wellbeing benefits, including focusing attention away from worries, exercise of lungs and can provide an enjoyable breathing and vocal activity and positivity.

Group Singing brings social support and belonging to all, and creates positive vibrations. Singing chants in praise of God, called Kirtan, is the highest form of collective singing which encourages reflection and togetherness besides the mind.

Dancing is an an enjoyable way to express emotions and stay physically active. Each of these enjoyable activities can be done at any age.
