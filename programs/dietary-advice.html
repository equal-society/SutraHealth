Dietary Advice & Healthy Eating Guidelines

Balanced nutrition and mindful eating habits can support overall health, energy, wellbeing and healthy daily living.

Healthy Living Guidelines

While every individual may need personalized guidance based on their Prakriti and health needs, the following recommendations can support healthy daily habits for many people.

Daily Routine

  • Have a regular routine of sleep, waking up, meals, and physical activity.
  • Consider maintaining consistent meal and sleep timings according to your individual routine and needs.
  • Choose water and suitable non-alcoholic fluids to maintain healthy hydration throughout the day.

Foods to Limit

Limiting highly processed foods and choosing a balanced variety of whole foods can support healthy eating habits and long-term wellbeing.

❌ Refined White Sugar

❌ Excess Salt

❌ Fried Foods

Samosa, Pakoda and other deep-fried snacks.

❌ Ultra-Processed Foods

Chips, packaged junk foods and highly processed snacks.

❌ Commercial Bread

❌ Highly Sweetened Tea

A hospital-based Cell Metabolism study on ultra-processed foods reported that an ultra-processed diet was associated with increased food intake and weight gain under the conditions studied.

Recommended Foods

Follow a diverse and balanced diet that includes a variety of nutritious foods to support everyday health and wellbeing.

🍎 Fruits & Natural Juices

🥗 Vegetables & Salads

Include a variety of seasonal vegetables.

🍵 Green Tea

🧈 Desi Ghee & Olive Oil

🌾 Gur & Khandsari

🥣 Pulses & Dals

🌿 Millets

Jowar and Ragi can be included as part of a varied diet.

🥛 Buttermilk

🥚 Eggs in Moderation

🐟 Fish

Choose protein sources according to your dietary preferences and individual needs.

Meal Frequency

Meal frequency can be adjusted according to individual preferences, routine, nutritional needs and medical advice.

Hydration & Fluids

Drink enough water throughout the day to support healthy hydration. Individual fluid requirements can vary depending on activity, climate, age and health circumstances.

Traditional beverages such as Kaada may be included according to individual preference and suitability.

Personalized Dietary Guidance

Consistently following balanced dietary habits may support positive changes in overall health and wellbeing.

For individualized dietary advice based on your health needs, preferences and body constitution, please seek guidance from a qualified healthcare professional.
