Guided Dhyana Meditation for Beginners

Explore a guided Dhyana meditation practice based on witness awareness, breath observation, Prana awareness and AUM. Practice gradually and comfortably.

DHYANA MEDITATION

What Is Dhyana Meditation?

Dhyana is a yogic meditation practice associated with sustained attention and meditative awareness. This page presents a guided practice that moves from neutral observation and breath awareness toward Prana and AUM-based practice.

Meditation for Beginners

Beginners can start by sitting comfortably, relaxing the body and observing the natural breath without forcing it. The aim is to develop steady awareness gradually.

Witness Awareness

Witness meditation involves observing thoughts, sensations and surroundings without immediately reacting to them. The practice below uses neutral observation as an important part of Dhyana.

Breath & AUM Awareness

The practice includes observation of breathing and an AUM-based closing practice. Follow the instructions gently and avoid forcing the breath.

Dhyana Meditation: Witness Awareness

  • Witness everything as a neutral observer.
  • Visualize Earth as a small dot in the vast universe.
  • Affirm: I am not the body; I am not the mind. I am the Spirit.
  • I do not need anything; I do nothing. Nothing can hurt me.
  • I am the spirit, everlasting joy.
  • Visualize everything neutrally as though a river is flowing, traffic is passing and life is moving like a movie.
  • Observe breath and heartbeat as a neutral observer.
  • Observe the breath slowing down naturally.
  • Become thought-less and detached from body consciousness.
  • Imagine yourself inside a capsule cut off from the outer world.
  • Let time stand still and feel yourself as the center of the universe.
  • Observe Ida (Left Nostril – Lunar Energy) and Pingla (Right Nostril – Solar Energy).

Prana Awareness and AUM Meditation

  • Press thumb against index finger.
  • Inhale Prana with tongue folded upward.
  • Imagine consciousness rising up the spine.
  • Visualize the Sun's energy entering your body.
  • Hold the breath and spread Prana throughout the body.
  • Feel warmth and energy radiating within.
  • Exhale with the "M" sound of AUM.
  • Imagine light showering all around you.
  • Relax the body completely.
  • Remain still and feel the vibrations of AUM.

How to Finish Your Meditation Practice

  • Rub both hands together.
  • Place warm palms over the eyes.
  • Feel the glow and positive energy.
  • Celebrate with joyful laughter.
  • Maintain a deep smile.
  • Express gratitude to the Lord.
  • Remain optimistic that good will happen.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Dhyana Meditation FAQs

Common questions about Dhyana, meditation for beginners and this guided practice.

What is Dhyana meditation?

Dhyana is a yogic meditation practice associated with sustained attention and meditative awareness. This page presents a specific guided Dhyana practice.

How do you practice Dhyana meditation?

Begin comfortably, settle the body, observe the breath and follow the guided witness-awareness and meditation instructions on this page.

How can beginners start meditation?

Beginners can start with a comfortable posture, relaxed breathing and short periods of quiet observation, gradually becoming familiar with the practice.

Can beginners practice Dhyana meditation?

Beginners can explore the practice gradually. If any instruction feels uncomfortable, stop and return to natural breathing or seek appropriate professional guidance.

What are the benefits of Dhyana meditation?

Meditation is commonly practiced for awareness, relaxation and inner stillness. Individual experiences can vary, and this page does not promise a specific medical outcome.

What is the difference between Dhyana and meditation?

Dhyana is a term from the yogic tradition used for a form of meditative practice. Meditation is a broader term covering many different practices.

What is witness meditation?

Witness meditation refers to observing thoughts, sensations or experiences with a neutral, non-reactive attitude rather than immediately engaging with them.

What is AUM meditation?

AUM meditation can involve attention to the sound and vibration of AUM. This page includes AUM within its guided Dhyana practice.

Can meditation help with stress and relaxation?

Meditation is often used as a relaxation and stress-management practice. It should not be presented as a guaranteed treatment or cure for a medical condition.

How long should a beginner meditate each day?

There is no single duration that suits everyone. Beginners can choose a manageable period and build a regular practice gradually without forcing themselves.
