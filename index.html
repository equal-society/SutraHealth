Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about lifestyle medicine and yoga therapy.

Lifestyle medicine uses evidence-informed approaches such as nutrition, physical activity, sleep, stress management, and healthy lifestyle practices to support prevention, management, and overall wellbeing. It can complement appropriate medical care for chronic conditions.

Yoga therapy and healthy lifestyle practices may support blood glucose and blood pressure management for some people. They should be used alongside appropriate medical care, and any medication changes should be discussed with your healthcare provider.
